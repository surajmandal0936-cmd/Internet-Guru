About Our Project

Internet Guru is an educational website created for the Community Engagement Project (CEP), titled "Teaching Internet Basics to First-Generation Learners". It helps beginners learn how to use the internet, search for information, send emails, and stay safe online.

Learn Internet Basics

🌍 What is the Internet?

The internet connects computers and mobile devices around the world. It helps us share information and communicate.

🔎 Searching Information

Search engines help us find information. Type your question or keywords into a search engine and review the results.

🌐 Websites and Browsers

A browser is an application used to open websites. Examples include Google Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Microsoft Edge.

✉️ Email Basics

Email allows us to send and receive messages, documents, photos, and other files online.

📱 Online Services

The internet helps us access educational resources, government services, online forms, and digital communication tools.

💻 Digital Learning

Students can use the internet to attend online classes, watch educational videos, and learn new skills.

Online Safety Tips

🔐 Strong Passwords

Create strong, unique passwords and never share them with strangers.

🔢 Protect Your OTP

Never share your OTP, PIN, or banking password with anyone.

⚠️ Avoid Fake Links

Do not click suspicious links or trust messages promising unexpected prizes.

💳 Safe Digital Payments

Check the receiver and amount before making a payment. Never reveal your UPI PIN.
